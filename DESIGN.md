# Design Specification: Cek Cuaca (Nordic Atmospheric Weather Observatory)

- **Product:** Live weather dashboard with global city search, browser geolocation, 24-hour temperature curve, and 7-day forecast powered by Open-Meteo (zero exposed API keys).
- **Visual Direction:** Nordic Atmospheric Observatory (Light default: Glacier Mist `#F2F5F8`, Crisp White `#FFFFFF`, Maritime Ink `#0F1E2E`, Barometer Steel `#1E3A5F`, Solar Amber `#9A4707`; Night Sky Dark Mode: `#0C131D`, Surface `#131E2C`, Sky Accent `#7DD3FC`). All 7 color pairs verified WCAG AA >= 4.5:1.
- **Typography:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` + `Space Mono` for crisp meteorological telemetry.
- **Three Dials (Antislop):** ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 2, MOTION 1.
